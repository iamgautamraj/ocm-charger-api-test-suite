# BUG-002 (data quality): Charger listed in Ahmedabad, India has a longitude outside India
Severity: Low-Medium
Test case: TC-07

## Steps to reproduce
GET https://api.openchargemap.io/v3/poi/?output=json&countrycode=IN&maxresults=100&key=<KEY>
Look for charger ID 510821.

## Expected
Coordinates inside India (latitude about 6-38, longitude about 68-98), consistent with the
stated town (Ahmedabad).

## Actual
ID 510821, "Nilkanth EV Charging Station - ChargeJet", Town: Ahmedabad,
Latitude 23.7593343, Longitude 53.3224671. The longitude places it outside India.
It was 1 of 100 records returned for countrycode=IN.

## Impact
The charger would be missing from location searches near Ahmedabad and drawn in the wrong
place on a map.
